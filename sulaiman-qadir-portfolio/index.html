ABOUT

MORE THAN
A PERSONAL
TRAINER.

Sulaiman Qadir is a Dubai-based sports performance professional whose work combines coaching, conditioning, movement quality and structured recovery.

With more than six years of fitness-industry experience, his professional focus spans personal training, sports conditioning, corrective exercise, sports massage, recovery coaching and practical sports nutrition.

His approach starts with understanding the individual. Training is selected for a reason, recovery is matched to physical demand, and progress is tracked so the programme evolves with the client.

“The goal is not to make every session harder. The goal is to make every session useful.”
